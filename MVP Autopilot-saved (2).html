MVP Blueprint Report

Target Audience:

  • Primary: Elderly individuals (65+) managing polypharmacy and individuals with chronic conditions.
  • Secondary: Caregivers, family members managing medication for dependents.
  • Tertiary: Temporary users recovering from surgery or acute illnesses.

Core Features (MVP):

  • Medication Scheduler: Input for name, dosage, frequency, and visual cues.
  • Smart Reminders: Critical-alert push notifications with persistent alarms, offering "Mark as Taken," "Snooze," or "Skip" options from the lock screen.
  • Adherence Log: Daily dashboard of scheduled vs. taken doses, with a history view.
  • Refill Tracker: Inventory management with automatic decrementing and "Low Supply" alerts.
  • Caregiver Link (Basic): Ability to export a weekly adherence log summary via text or email.

Technology Stack:

  • Cross-Platform Framework: Flutter or React Native.
  • Backend-as-a-Service (BaaS): Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions).
  • Local Services: SQLite / Hive for offline persistence, Local Notification API.

Monetization Strategy:

Freemium Model:

  • Free Tier: Core functionality, local notifications, refill tracking, supported by banner ads.
  • Premium Subscription ($4.99/mo or $39.99/yr): Ad removal, Cloud Sync, Caregiver Live-Mode (real-time notifications for missed doses), Doctor Reports (PDF adherence history).

App Design Document

Executive Summary: UI/UX design focuses on accessibility, clarity, and trust, prioritizing high contrast, large touch targets, and cognitive ease for the primary demographic (65+), while maintaining a modern aesthetic.

Design Philosophy & UX Principles:

  • Accessibility First (WCAG 2.1 AA Compliance): Scaled UI elements, generous touch targets, and cognitive ease through single-action screens and wizard-style flows. Haptic and audio feedback for confirmation.
  • Error Prevention: "Fat-finger" tolerance with generous padding (min 48x48dp tappable area) and two-step verification or long-press for destructive actions.

Visual Identity:

  • Mood Board Keywords: Clinical, Warm, Reliable, Organized, Serene.
  • Imagery: Clean, vector-based illustrations.
  • Shape Language: Rounded corners (12-16px radius) for softness and safety.

Logo Concepts:

Stylized clock face with a pill, or a shield icon with a checkmark.

Color Palette:

  • Primary: MediMinder Blue (#2D9CDB) for trust and professionalism; Canvas White (#FAFAFA) for reduced glare.
  • Functional: Action Green (#27AE60) for positive actions; Alert Coral (#EB5757) for missed doses/low supply; Neutral Grey (#828282) for secondary text.
  • Dark Mode Support: Deep Gunmetal (#121212) background, Dark Grey (#1E1E1E) surface, 90% White text.

Typography:

  • Typeface: Inter or Roboto (Google Fonts).
  • Sizes: Display (24-32pt Bold), Body (18pt Regular/Medium), Caption/Label (14pt Medium).
  • Dynamic Type: App respects OS-level font scaling up to 200% without layout breakage.

UI Components & Feature Design:

  • Navigation (Thumb Zone): Bottom Navigation Bar with labels: "Today," "Meds," "History," "Profile."
  • Medication Scheduler (Input): Visual pill selector (icon + color) and frequency wheels for time selection.
  • Smart Reminders (Alert Screen): Full-screen overlay with pill icon, name, dosage, scheduled time, and three stacked buttons: "TAKE NOW" (Green), "SNOOZE 15M" (Blue Outline), "SKIP" (Grey Text Link).
  • Adherence Log & Dashboard: Vertical timeline for "Today" view (Upcoming, Taken, Missed). Calendar view for History with color-coded days (Green, Yellow, Red).
  • Refill Tracker & Premium Features: Circular progress bar for supply (Green > 20%, Red < 20%). Premium features are greyed out with a gold crown icon.
  • Caregiver Mode (Export): Report preview and simple traffic light status indicators.
